📖 Naming convention & piglet categories

Houses & pens: A1–A14, B1–B16, C1–C17, D1–D20, E1–E20, F1–F20, EM1–EM4 (49 pens total).

Sow tags: 3-digit numbers (001–199), starting at 100 for new tags. Existing 3-digit tags (029, 053, 070, 077, 082, 085, 086, 088, 089, 090, 094, 097) stay.

Boars: "BR" prefix (BR01–BR99) to keep visually distinct.

Litter ID: L-[sow tag]-[YYMM] e.g. L-089-2606 = sow 089's litter farrowed June 2026.

Retainers: [sow tag]-[YY][letter] e.g. 089-26A = sow 089's first retained daughter from a 2026 litter.

Slaughter pigs: No individual tags — tracked at pen level via litter ID notch on day 1; slap tattoo applied at FC delivery truck.
